09:00–09:15OpeningOpening RemarksSophia AnaniadouThe University of Manchester

Daniel Preoțiuc-Pietro is the engineering manager of the AI Search Services Group at Bloomberg, New York City. His group develops the search models, tools and infrastructure that power both low-latency search and conversational use cases with access to hundreds of millions of unstructured documents across the Bloomberg Terminal.

His research encompasses topics in NLP, LLMs and Computational Social Science, where he published over 50 top-tier publications. Primary current research areas include introducing new task setups, applications and NLP resources inspired by industry use cases. Personal research interests focus on analyzing the pragmatic, social and temporal dimensions of text, particularly from social media, with cross-disciplinary applications in psychology, law, political science and journalism. This research has been featured extensively in popular press including The Washington Post, BBC and New Scientist.

He co-founded and co-organizes the Natural Legal Language Processing (NLLP) workshop series collocated with *ACL conferences and serves as Industry Track Chair for EACL 2027. Prior to joining Bloomberg, he was a postdoctoral research fellow at the University of Pennsylvania and obtained a PhD in the NLP Group at the University of Sheffield, UK.

16:45–17:15PanelWhy FinNLP — and Who Is It For?Moderated by Chung-Chi Chenwith the invited speakers

Why does financial NLP merit being a field in its own right, what problems is it uniquely placed to address, and who ultimately benefits? Beyond the usual research and industry framing, the panel turns to the broader public value of the field: financial transparency, reducing information asymmetry, supporting regulators and journalists, protecting consumers, and making complex financial information accessible. And the harder question: if much of FinNLP helps financial institutions operate or invest more effectively, what should the wider contribution of the field be?
